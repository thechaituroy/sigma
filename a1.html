About this course

In A1 German, you learn to understand and use basic phrases, introduce yourself, ask and answer simple questions, and handle everyday situations in German.

What you will learn

  • Personal information (name, age, nationality, profession)
  • Numbers, dates, time
  • Family and relationships
  • Daily routines
  • Food and Beverages
  • Shopping and clothing
  • Directions and transport
  • Weather and seasons
  • Hobbies and leisure
  • Health and the body
  • Vacations and city tours
  • Friends and free time activities

Grammar

  • Nouns and articles (der, die, das; plurals)
  • Personal pronouns (ich, du, er, sie, etc.)
  • Verb conjugation (regular and common irregular verbs: sein, haben, gehen, etc.)
  • Present tense
  • Negation (nicht, kein)
  • Modal verbs (können, müssen, wollen – in simple contexts)
  • Simple questions (W-questions: wer, was,wen, wo, wann; and yes/no questions)
  • Word order (main clauses, basic inversion with questions)
  • Prepositions (am, im, mit, zu, etc. – in simple phrases)
  • Possessive pronouns (mein, dein, sein, etc.)
  • Perfekt Tense
  • Trennbare und Untrennbare Verben
  • Präteritum von haben und sein Verben

A1 Exam Structure (Goethe, TELC, ÖSD formats)

Listening

  • Understand familiar words and simple sentences (e.g. announcements, short conversations)
  • Topics: at the supermarket, public transport, phone messages, etc.

Reading

  • Match advertisements and notices
  • Emails, short personal messages or a postcard
  • Short paragraph or public information

Writing

  • Fill out forms (name, address, etc.)
  • Write a short message or email (e.g. invitation, apology, thank you note)

Speaking

  • Part 1: Introduce yourself (name, origin, age, profession)
  • Part 2: Ask and answer simple questions (e.g. about hobbies, food, daily life, travel, work)
  • Part 3: Make requests, respond to prompts (using pictures/word cards)

COURSE OUTCOMES

  • Introduce yourself and others
  • Ask and answer basic questions
  • Use simple sentences to describe familiar things
  • Interact in a simple way if the other person speaks slowly and clearly.
