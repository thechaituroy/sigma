Biography

As a dedicated German language instructor, I specialize in preparing students for the Goethe-Zertifikat A1 and A2 exams. Since 2023, I've successfully managed large batches of over 40 students per session, training approximately 100 students every three months. My teaching approach is centered on creating a structured and supportive learning environment that enables students to approach their exams with confidence and ease. In addition to group sessions, I offer one-on-one and accelerated group sessions for those who prefer a more personalized learning path. I am committed to not just teaching the language, but also to motivating and guiding students to stay enthusiastic and committed throughout their learning journey.
